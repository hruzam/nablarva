## Candidate graduation, not four approved builds

1. One bed, one host, operator-opened sessions: show the run's participants and let the
   operator explicitly attach each selected full session to an exact endpoint.
2. Prepare/preview the recipient and artifact context; prove one operator-triggered,
   correlated request/response path for one pairing when separately authorized.
3. Earn recovery and any unattended behavior through negative tests and an explicit policy
   decision. An ambiguous outcome stays unknown and stops automatic repetition.
4. Only then consider more pairings, cross-host operation, or a broader surface.

The relationship to a head belongs to run topology; a transport connector should not assume
every endpoint is a Claude-shaped team leader. Keep role, bed, host, workspace, runtime and
session/incarnation distinct. Explicit local binding can be useful without first solving a
global identity ontology. These are design constraints and recommendations, not a locked schema.
