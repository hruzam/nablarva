## Adding a design

New material: `meshup/<slug>/{DESIGN.md, HYPOTHESES.md, raw/}` — `raw/` only for files born inside that design or copied in with an origin header. Substrate that predates the registry stays in `raw.nablarva/` and is cited. Dated research goes to `.dev/research/<slug>/`. A sub-hypothesis or small shift of an existing design is a **phase**, not a new slug. Duplicate/cross-interference sweep before any slug lands (AGENTS.md naming law).
