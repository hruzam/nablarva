## 6. Alternatives — the document's shape (G1 direction stands)

### A — Spine (the L16.9 list, in order)
`shape of record · layer model · dependency direction · shared grammars · cross-organ ownership ·
system-wide boundaries · ladder · organ list · decisions · open questions`

- **Strength:** it matches L16.9 and the prompt item by item. It is the easiest to check.
- **Cost:** ownership and boundaries are written separately about the same seams, which tends to fork facts
  (L16.10). Accepted vs planned has no fixed place. v0's principles would scatter across four sections.

### B — Seam ledger (L16.9 content; ownership + boundaries keyed by seam)
`shape of record · layer model (whole animal, with v0's six layers as the brick-layer sub-model) ·
dependency direction · principles (v0 candidates, §2a) · SEAM LEDGER: seam · owner · consumers ·
lock · accepted · planned · open — rows: observe · compose · deliver/write-into-session ·
view-layout & process lifetime · room truth · human door · cross-host · checkout coordination ·
context sourcing/reading · key meanings · shared grammars (KEYS CODE; v0 rule 7) · ladder (by
reference to rows) · organ list · decisions & reading schedule · open questions (X1–X13, X2-1..3)`

- **Strength:** v0's collisions are all seam collisions. X1, X9 and X10 are on deliver, X8 on lifetime, X4 and X11 on room
  truth and observe. A row with fixed accepted / planned / open cells shows them in one place, with each
  cell's tag checkable. The X2 seam becomes one row (observe) that X2 can read directly, and the VIST and v0 candidates appear
  as candidate cells, not as decisions.
- **Cost:** cells compress. Nuance must live in short notes pointing to the owning source. It departs
  from L16.9's listing order, not its content. The first review is larger.

### C — Principles-first (v0-shaped, re-grounded on locks)
`idea · principles · layers · tree and substrate · substrate & events (as open questions) ·
messages & delivery · organs · decisions-with-revisit · ladder · open questions`

- **Strength:** it is the reading shape Majkee commissioned (v0), so it is easy for him to read. The
  decisions-with-revisit table is native to it.
- **Cost / risk:** v0's sections are brick-layer and implementation-flavoured. Whole-animal parts (the ladder,
  stridularium, cross-host, the observer) fit badly. The "substrate & events" and "messages" sections pull hardest toward
  manufacturing docket 5/8 and the X1 mechanism. `inferred: houston`: this is the shape most likely to promote v0's details,
  which Majkee explicitly said not to insist on.

(arc.md's ladder-staged shape is retired. v0 does not improve it, and stages 2–3 are still mostly
`unknown:`.)

### Lean — `inferred: houston`

**B, plus one borrowing from v0:** the decisions/defaults section uses v0's
"decision · reason · revisit when" columns. That honours v0 as the frame Majkee named, together with K8 and G7's no-cage
qualification, without importing v0's details. A is a sound literal choice. C reads best but
