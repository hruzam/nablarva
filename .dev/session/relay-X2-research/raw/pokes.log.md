# pokes.log — relay-X2-research

Every poke of the lead, one entry each, written by the lead (claude 689d87d0-6d49-4c5c-ae33-7fe561e92ac8, pane %70).
Three things are recorded apart and never folded into one (harvest row U20): what the carrier answered, when the watch
ends, and when the RETURN was seen. A later arrival is added to its entry; the original watch deadline stays as written.
Exit 0 shows that the queue accepted the call, nothing more.
