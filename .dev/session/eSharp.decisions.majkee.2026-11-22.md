## 2. My answer — yes, if the manager holds no state

**The manager's job, three duties**
- Navigation only: one small board that points at each group's STATUS and lists what one group needs from another. It never writes
  into a group's bed. Each head stays its own status owner.
- One queue to Majkee: it collects the heads' decision sheets, orders them, and hands them to the desk, so he is asked once per
  sitting, most urgent first.
- Resource notices: when a group produces something the relay lead can use, the manager sends him a pointer.

**Six points**
1. A separate tmux server for the helper-window group. Rearranging panes inside `helloWorld` while the probe stage measures tmux
   paste and submit there would spoil the measurement.
2. One decision desk, not two. The existing exec desk serves all lines; each sheet names its line.
3. One shared checkout: path-scoped commits are mandatory, and one seat owns the push.
4. The line against myShell: she keeps continuity across his tasks; the manager navigates between the lines inside nablarva.
5. A staggered start: the helper-window group first, the shared-terminal group once the first has closed a cycle.
6. Group 2's Claude auditor: the `trajectory` build fits a re-checking seat better than `flight`, which is told to plan and lead.

**On "fresher head".** After a clear the manager is a new session that reads files. So the two RUNBOOKs, the briefs and the
manager's own board and card are the carrier. The brief says the manager verifies nothing a head has already verified.

**What I need before writing two RUNBOOKs.** A gate line for each group; the substrate for the shared terminal as paths outside
his vault; whether the helper group continues `session-window-X0-substrate` or opens a new bed.
