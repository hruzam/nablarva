# A confirmed summary still needs the operator's own line beside it

**Observed (cycle 10).** The walkthrough ran eleven themes in about four hours. For T1–T5 the desk quoted Majkee verbatim. From T6
on, by his own rule R1, the desk wrote a summary after he confirmed its reading. One of those summaries (T9, pushes) can be read as
the opposite of the sentence he typed, and his confirmation was a single "yes" to a message that held two questions. The pen
holder could only find this by opening the desk's session log.

**What worked.** One theme per turn. His habit of asking "please explain" until the question was clear. Quotes for T1–T5 matched
his typed words 15 of 15.

**Proposals**
1. Keep R1, and add the operator's reply as one quoted line under each confirmed summary. It costs one line and lets the next
   reader check the summary without a session log.
2. One question per confirmation. If a message ends with two questions, a "yes" answers neither for the record.
3. At most three parts per theme on the sheet. T6 and T10 had five; parts were skipped or merged.
4. A decision that changes a standing permission (who may push, who may send) gets its own turn and its own quote.
