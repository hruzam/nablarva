# G5 — U03–U06: one candidate receiver contract and oracle sheet

**Why:** expiry, wrong recipient, action authority and hop/retry limits all test one thing: the receiver's decision rule. They need one
written contract and one oracle table, not four projects.

**Done when** one document answers the four questions the packet left open:
1. what identifies a logical request, its recipient and its de-duplication domain;
2. which clock and expiry reading apply, and which path/action scope;
3. what a pointer authorises, and what needs a separate native permission;
4. which clean failures permit a retry, and how hop and attempt budgets end;

and for each of U03–U06 gives the expected receiver decision, an oracle that does not depend on the actor, the fixture it needs and a
finite budget (start from the matrix's candidate bounds). Reviewed by a non-author. Marked **candidate**: no envelope, owner or
transport is adopted.

**Order note:** the X0 authors put observation first (L20) and kept receiver policy as a separate appendix. So this comes after G3/G4
unless Majkee reorders.
