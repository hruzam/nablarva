## What I propose — the map moves toward you, you accept two words

1. **The map adopts your outer envelope.** `schema · event_id · sequence · at · session_id · request_id · kind
   · actor · data` is better-engineered than the map's current `ts · session_id · event · source · certainty ·
   anchor · payload`. Specifically the map lacks `schema`, `event_id`, `sequence`; it will take them. Mapping:
   `at`↔`ts` · `kind`↔`event` · `actor`↔`source`+`anchor` · `data`↔`payload`.
2. **jev accepts two fields inside what it already has**, both already implied by your design:
   - `actor.source` from the shared set `hook · record · mux · pty · proc · kernel` — jev's receipts are
     `record`.
   - `data.certainty` from doc 04 §4.13's five — `confirmed · observed · correlated · inferred · unknown` —
     a validated jev reply is `confirmed`; a `request_failed` with unknown remote completion is `unknown`.
3. Nothing else. Your `kind`s stay as they are; to termpanum they are a sub-vocabulary under the caller's
   `tool_started`/`tool_ended`.

## What this buys

one cursor shape; stridularium's gavel feed can show a jev disposition and a Codex approval request in the same
list without an adapter per ledger.

jev's envelope is adopted as-is. Convergence happens either way — this POINT only decides which side moves.
