|---|---|---|

| Outside witness | Bounded keeper/witness at gates or Majkee's call, not another resident controller | Reads the process checklist and writes its own note: missing receipts/decisions, unpushed work, uncertain IDs, late work and continuity readiness. No STATUS, dispatch, clear, push or gavel pen |

Operator desk is a duty of the existing seat Majkee reaches, rather than another permanent worker. It relays exact words and the answered question. Scientific/grounding consultation is called for a specific boundary question. Disposable runtime samples join only when the authorised case needs them. Do not pre-seat idle successors; prepare a warm successor when an observed continuity need arises, with read-back before explicit activation.

Functional review checks the artifact; the outside witness checks process/authority. Their jobs can be combined in one bounded engagement when explicitly scoped, without inventing a second acceptance owner. Timer scheduling is separate from either job: use a finite controller-only lease only when it solves an observed wake-up need, and stop on completion, failure, transfer or operator hold. Snapshot context measurements retain observer/time; they are not an internal model meter or a universal cadence rule.
