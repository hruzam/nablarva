# Six inherited criteria — fixtures, independent oracles and release boundaries

This is a preparation matrix, **not an executable test contract or a completed qualification**.
`carryover` preserves original expectations; `cases` and `inventory` provide historical evidence.
Those IDs resolve to exact commits/hashes and readable archive paths in the source-pin JSON.
Future owners below are **proposed responsibilities**, not appointed runtime seats or settled
cross-organ ownership. Every case needs actual endpoint/interface/version and fixture binding,
frozen expected mapping, authority and an independent reviewer before execution.

Candidate finite bounds below make the proposed workload reviewable. They are not adopted policy
or a release. A future preregistration must either explicitly adopt them or propose reviewed new
bounds **before** a run. Native approval is a hold, not a timeout to bypass. Absence of side effects
is claimed only for the declared observed interval, never forever.
