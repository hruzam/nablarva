- **Vendor difference is useful decorrelation, not a measured superiority result.** R1–R6 demonstrate the value of this independent review. We ran no controlled comparison proving cross-vendor review better than a suitably independent same-vendor reviewer. Select for independent judgment and the actual output contract.
- **The successor was warm.** I had already reviewed the report. POINT17 read-back, pushed activation18 and old-head retirement gave one observed unambiguous pen transfer. Do not call this zero-history reincarnation or claim nothing was lost: that was not measured. A pane footer can be observed externally at a timestamp; it does not mean the model has a live internal context meter. Preserve who observed it and when.

I completed the inventory without arming another timer. Keeper RETURN12 exists and gives watch/advice; the hook refusal does not erase that contribution. - **Permissions should follow observed actions, not vendor stereotypes.** The Nov12 Trajectory record allowed pane input and push; Nov13 compound-command prompts remain explicitly unverified operator/author accounts. Do not turn that into a universal “Claude files, Codex shell” boundary or a broad mandatory allow-list. ## The smallest team I would start with

1. **One resident controller/integration owner.** Cartan/Sol is a demonstrated option in this run, not a lock. This seat holds the one pen, preserves the original operator gate and knows when it is author versus reviewer. It need not run every transport or keep a timer armed.
2. **One resident builder/report author.** Trajectory/Opus is a demonstrated option. Give whole, bounded work and its exact file scope. The builder's safe shell capability is determined by real permissions, not excluded by its brand. Operator-desk relay can be a named duty of whichever existing seat Majkee uses on mobile, without a fourth permanent controller.
3. **An independent reviewer engaged at acceptance edges.** Use a peer or bounded worker with the necessary read access and a known durable-result writer. If the role is read-only, plan its publisher explicitly; never assume it can write its RETURN. If the head authored an artifact, the head cannot supply its independent acceptance.

Add a bounded scientific/grounding consultation when the question needs it; keep it outside lifecycle and verdict ownership. Keep an existing hotline available by a named brief rather than waking it for every cycle. Add disposable Claude/Codex samples only when the authorised case needs those runtimes. I would not pre-seat a permanent spare successor or automatic keeper by default; a warm successor can be prepared when a measured context/continuity need appears. These are staffing suggestions for Majkee/the X0 head, not assignments made here.
