## Curvature

Majkee's "resources pipes" may describe the desired future facility, but no
ovitmugen payload pipe exists in the inspected current source. Its named tmux
server sockets are control endpoints, not an application exchange protocol.
Termbrana's real pipe belongs to a probe, not a reusable product transport.

`ov-ls --json` is useful discovery data but not sufficient routing authority:
bed_report omits explicit host/socket identity and pane identity in its emitted
record. Its window IDs and busy flags do not authenticate a recipient. Tmux.run
uses text-mode command output; it is not a raw packet-byte delivery API.

## Uncertainty disposition

Cartan's provisional recommendation is Majkee's layering B: both tools consume
a small independently owned lower boundary where reuse is justified, rather than
Termbrana inheriting the dashboard lifecycle. Ovitmugen remains views/layout;
Termbrana composition remains independent of a session bed/editor. This does not
claim a shared layer exists or create it now. Exact shared API, writer, target
binding, payload lifetime, delivery acknowledgement and carrier qualification
remain design work. Current discovery-only use can be qualified separately.
