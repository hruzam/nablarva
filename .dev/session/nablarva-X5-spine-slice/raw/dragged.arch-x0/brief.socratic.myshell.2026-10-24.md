Complementary Socratic brief for architecture-X0-spine-and-skin.

Read the CS card and:
~/unikuklatrix/nablarva/.dev/session/nablarva-X2-builder-narative/raw/05_WAY_OF_THINKING.md

Keep current flag authority and both predecessor handoffs visible.
Their recommendations are inputs to examine.

Use the questions below as thinking lenses. Answer what existing evidence
already answers. Bring Majkee only the missing intent or consequential
tradeoffs, one manageable question at a time.

1. What useful whole are we building?
   Describe one concrete workflow Majkee should complete through Nablarva.
   What becomes easier, more reliable or newly possible?
   What observable result would demonstrate that improvement?

2. Which uncertainty determines the next move?
   Is it observation fidelity, coordination, human interaction, ownership,
   or something else? What evidence would change our proposed priority?
   Test "spine before skin" against this question.

3. What should be shared, and why?
   Which capabilities benefit from common process/API/CLI/subscription
   layers under L21? Which clients must remain independently useful?
   What actual dependency requires runtime coupling?

4. What exists today?
   Separate accepted constraints, implemented capabilities, qualified
   behavior and proposed capabilities. Which dependency is supported
   only by a name, document or planned component?

5. What does each observation establish?
   Distinguish source bytes, rendered screen, local record, inferred
   meaning and explicit acknowledgement.
   Where could stillness be mistaken for readiness, or disappearance
   for acceptance? How will uncertainty remain visible?

6. Where does truth live?
   Which records are source evidence, which are projections, and which
   constitute room truth? Who writes each?
   Can records be replayed and interpreted without the original viewer?

7. Who owns lifetime and effects?
   Who starts, stops and recovers the source, collector and viewer?
   What survives viewer closure?
   Where do observation, policy decisions and authorized actions meet?
   Which exact command path performs each consequential effect?

8. What happens between successful steps?
   Consider source restart, stale identity, collector failure, partial
   writes and uncertain action outcomes.
   What could appear successful while losing evidence or duplicating
   an effect? How would we detect it?

9. What does the human need to understand?
   What must the interface reveal before a person decides or acts?
   Which interactions distinguish inspect, prepare, execute and request
   delivery? Which requirements should inform the skin/UI discussion
   before choosing a framework?

10. What is already understood outside Nablarva?
    For the selected seam, apply all four questions from 05:
    conventional names; established solutions and failure modes;
    remaining practitioner problems; what is particular to our case.
    Keep research bounded to a question that changes the decision.

11. What would falsify our preferred approach?
    Who defines expected results independently of its implementation?
    Preserve the accepted Q qualification work and distinguish its
    documentary acceptance from permission to execute.
    What would make us stop, narrow the claim or choose another approach?

12. What is the smallest useful next commitment?
    What uncertainty does it remove, what inspectable evidence remains,
    and what does it deliberately leave open?
    Can that result be useful before a shared envelope, dashboard or
    whole-animal runtime exists?
