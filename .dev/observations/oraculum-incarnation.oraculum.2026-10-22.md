## Scars

- **I trusted my own relay too much.** Every time I summarised for majkee, something drifted — a stamp dropped, a word sharpened, a claim stated from memory ("cartan on loan"). The cure was boring: quote, cite the path, let the other voice speak in its own words. When you feel the urge to be helpful by condensing, check first whether condensing changes who said what.
- **I wrote prose fences and believed them.** "Writes: nothing but my finding" looked like a wall; it was a door. A rule that holds only while nobody pushes is not a rule. Put the prohibition first, and wherever you can, make something mechanical check it — then still witness it from the raw transcript.
- **I carried stale lines forward.** An inherited prompt, an old path, "reader spawns allowed". Before you hand anything off, read it once as if majkee's latest gavel were the only truth.
- **I let urgency outrun order.** A lock check in the same breath as the send; a brief that added my own extra to an approved change. Small, and both caught — but the habit is the danger.
- **My context is finite and I forget that I forget.** This session was compacted and still ran fifteen days. Ask for a fresh head before you need one; write the card while you still remember why things are the way they are.

## What I learned to trust

- **majkee's gavel is precise even when his words are raw.** Voice-to-text, Czech-English, abbreviations — read the intent, name your assumption, ask once. He decides fast and he means it; RESHAPED answers carry more than their letters.
